READING YOUR RESULT

A label. A score.
Some important distinctions.

THE LABEL

REAL or FAKE

The single-image workflow compares the model score with a fixed threshold of 0.1. Scores at or above that threshold receive FAKE. Lower scores receive REAL.

THE SCORE

A model output

The score runs from 0 to 1. Higher values indicate a stronger manipulation signal for this model. It is not a measured probability that the image is fake, or a confidence guarantee.

THE FACE COUNT

What was found

The face count tells you how many faces the pipeline detected. A missed face, small crop, or difficult angle can limit the analysis. Review per-face scores when available.

The folder-analysis tool has its own configurable threshold, defaulting to 0.5. Its labels can differ from single-image labels for the same score. This reflects existing application behavior.

WHERE CAUTION MATTERS

Every detector has blind spots.

Image degradation

Screenshots, heavy compression, and repeated edits can remove details or introduce artifacts that complicate analysis.

New manipulation methods

Patterns learned from one dataset do not guarantee performance on newer generators or unfamiliar image sources.

Missing context

A model cannot tell you whether the caption is truthful, the image was shared with consent, or the story has been corroborated.
