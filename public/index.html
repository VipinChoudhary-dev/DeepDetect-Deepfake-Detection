BEFORE YOU HIT ANALYZE

A few things worth knowing.

Can this prove an image is fake?

No. The model may flag authentic images or miss manipulated ones. Treat the output as a reason to investigate further.

Does this work on video or audio?

This workspace analyzes still images. Video and audio detection are not supported.

Why does image quality matter?

Compression, resizing, filters, and small or obscured faces can change the signals available to the detector.

Can the assistant see my uploaded image?

No. The assistant receives the text you send, not your image or scan results. It can explain general concepts, but cannot independently verify your photo.
