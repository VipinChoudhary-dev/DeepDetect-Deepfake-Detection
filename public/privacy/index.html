How this application handles analysis and AI conversations.

Image analysis

Image scanning is unavailable in this public preview. This static site does not accept or upload images.

Assistant conversations

Your typed question is sent through the application’s server to Google Gemini to generate a response. The assistant does not automatically receive your uploaded image, scan score. Messages displayed in the page are held for the current page session; clearing the conversation removes the visible messages, not any provider-side records.

The current assistant answers each question independently. Include relevant, non-sensitive context in your question. Do not send passwords, private identifiers, or confidential information.

What the results mean

Outputs are intended for research and education. Neither the classifier nor the assistant establishes authenticity. Verify important claims with independent sources, and do not use these outputs alone to accuse someone or make consequential decisions.

About DeepDetect

Made by Vipin Choudhary. Designed to make facial manipulation analysis easier to explore and understand.

The interactive face uses Google MediaPipe’s canonical facial geometry, distributed under Apache 2.0. It is a visual illustration, separate from the detection pipeline.
